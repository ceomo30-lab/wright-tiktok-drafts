# TikTok review demo script

Target: 3-5 minutes, MP4 or MOV, under 50 MB. Record the production-matching web UI on its submitted domain while using the TikTok sandbox.

1. Open the public Wright Drafts homepage. Show visible Terms and Privacy links without opening a menu.
2. Tap Connect TikTok. Show the TikTok authorization screen requesting `user.info.basic`, `video.publish`, and `video.upload`; authorize the sandbox target account.
3. Return to Wright Drafts. Show the connected TikTok username/nickname from creator info.
4. Open one finished carousel. Swipe through every slide and show that the exact order is visible.
5. Edit the caption.
6. Open privacy. Show that there is no default and that only current creator-info options appear. Select one.
7. Show comments off by default. For a photo post, do not show Duet or Stitch.
8. Turn commercial content on. Show Your brand and Branded content, their labels, the missing-choice blocker, and the private/branded-content restriction.
9. Show recommended auto-music and the exact TikTok Music Usage Confirmation text.
10. Show the proposed schedule and exact-post approval checkbox. Demonstrate that the final button stays disabled until all required choices and consent are complete.
11. Approve a SELF_ONLY sandbox test post. Show processing and the final status.
12. Open the multi-post queue. Show two different finished sandbox examples, each with its own preview, caption, privacy, disclosure, time, and checkbox. Explain that one final confirm records the checked exact versions, while each is submitted separately at its approved time.
13. Demonstrate a changed-version blocker by changing one queued example after review and showing that approval must be repeated.
14. Show Upload to TikTok as an optional fallback and explain that it sends an inbox draft rather than publishing.
15. Show Disconnect and revoke access, then show the signed-out state.

Do not show secrets, access tokens, client keys, client secrets, vaults, or private developer-account screens in the recording.
