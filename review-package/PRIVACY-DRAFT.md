# Wright Drafts Privacy Policy - owner-review draft

**Effective date:** [OWNER TO APPROVE]

This policy explains how Wright Drafts handles information when creators connect TikTok and prepare posts.

## Information handled
- TikTok account identifiers and profile information authorized through Login Kit, such as open ID, username, nickname, and avatar.
- OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to provide connected features.
- Carousels, captions, settings, disclosures, schedules, approvals, and publish status.
- Basic security and diagnostic records, such as timestamps, request outcomes, and abuse-prevention logs.

Wright Drafts does not ask for or store your TikTok password.

## How information is used
Information is used to connect the account, show the correct creator and current posting options, host and preview creator content, record exact approvals, send approved posts to TikTok, track status, support revocation, secure the service, and troubleshoot failures.

## Sharing
Information is sent to TikTok when needed for features the creator requests. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process information to operate the service. Wright Drafts does not sell personal information or use creator content to train advertising profiles. [OWNER MUST CONFIRM THESE COMMITMENTS AND NAME OR CATEGORIZE ACTUAL PROVIDERS.]
